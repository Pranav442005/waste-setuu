Who We Are

Bridging the Gap in Waste Management

Waste Setu is designed to create a structured digital connection between households, businesses, waste collectors, recyclers, manufacturers and waste processing organizations.

The word Setu means bridge. Waste Setu acts as a digital bridge between people who generate waste and organizations that can collect, segregate, recycle, reuse or process it.

Our goal is to make waste management more organized, accessible, transparent and efficient through dedicated waste portals and stakeholder connectivity.

One Platform. Multiple Connections.

Waste Setu brings different participants of the waste management ecosystem together through a common digital platform.

Waste Collectors Pickup, segregation and coordination
